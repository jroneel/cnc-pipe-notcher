02 / Cutting & bevel

B tilts the torch toward +X for positive angles. Bevel is relative to the radial direction; it does not follow the local notch normal. Amber shows an illustrative inner-wall allowance.

Z is the pivot distance from the tube centre. Enter the measured arm length; bevel export requires a nonzero value.

Preview first. Export supports axial/radial B bevels on configured grblHAL. Never run an unverified program on a machine.

    Machine assumptions

    X follows the tube; A rotates it about X; B tilts the torch toward X. Z positions the tilt pivot above the tube centre, including radial stock height and pivot compensation. No Y axis is used. A zero corresponds to the model's +Y surface at the head. Output uses inverse-time feed to coordinate millimetres and degrees.

    Current scope: one external end contour per operation. Interior holes, rounded stock corners, kerf compensation, pierce strategy, travel limits and arbitrary bevels require additional CAM and machine configuration.
